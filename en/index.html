Not just raw text, but ready to hand in

Speech recognition is only the start. Scrivolo mainly helps you check, correct and export.

Runs entirely on your PC

Recognition runs locally, even in airplane mode. The speech model is downloaded once as a data file, or imported by you.

Review marks

Uncertain passages are marked orange, likely hallucinated ones red. F3 jumps to the next one.

Editor with audio

Click a timestamp, read along, pause with Ctrl+Space. Split and merge segments, undo with Ctrl+Z.

Speakers

Assign and rename speakers, get speaker-change suggestions after pauses, use Alt+1…9 as shortcuts.

Custom terms

Enter names and technical terms once so they are spelled correctly.

Anonymisation

Replace names and places from a list, e.g. with [Name]. See every match at a glance.

Export for analysis

Word with line numbers for quoting, TXT with timestamps for MAXQDA and f4, subtitles as SRT.

Automatic backup

Saved every 20 seconds. After a crash, Scrivolo offers to pick up where you left off.

Long recordings

Hours-long files with thousands of segments stay smooth. Noise reduction kicks in automatically for noisy recordings.

Good to know: Scrivolo captures, analyses and exports. It does not provide legal, tax, medical or financial advice. Whether you may transcribe a recording (consent, your institution's data protection rules) is up to you to check. Recognition is not error-free; review the transcript before use.

How it works

  1. 1

    Load a recording

    Drag an audio file onto the window, choose language and accuracy.

  2. 2

    Let it transcribe

    Scrivolo recognises the text locally on your PC. The first 15 minutes of every file are free.

  3. 3

    Review and export

    Press F3 to go from mark to mark, set speakers, then export as Word, TXT or SRT.

FAQ

Who listens to my recordings?

Nobody but you. Scrivolo sends no recordings, transcripts or usage data to us or anyone else. There is no account and no telemetry.

Why does Scrivolo need the internet?

Only for the one-time download of the speech model (a pure data file) and for purchases via the Microsoft Store. Offline, you can import a model file instead.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Depending on the model, one hour of audio takes a few minutes to about an hour on a current PC; the app shows an estimate before you start.

How accurate is it?

That depends heavily on the recording, microphone and speakers. That is why Scrivolo marks uncertain passages so you can check them specifically.

Is Scrivolo GDPR compliant?

Scrivolo processes everything locally. Whether that is sufficient for your project is for you or your institution to decide; we do not give legal advice.
